## About This Site

Stridemind is an iOS app that uses dual-task walking training, designed to help
reduce fall risk and support cognitive health in adults aged 55 and older.

Dual-task walking training = performing a cognitive exercise (number recall,
pattern recognition, memory challenges) while walking simultaneously. This trains
the brain-body coordination that breaks down in older adults and leads to falls.

## Evidence Base

The approach is backed by clinical research:
- 44 randomised controlled trials, 2,782 older adults, pooled in a 2025
  European Geriatric Medicine systematic review and meta-analysis
- Confirmed outcomes: significantly improved dynamic balance and functional
  mobility
- Less certain outcome: reduced fall frequency, based on only 6 of those 44
  trials, all using self-reported falls
- Supporting: Cochrane 2019 found balance and functional exercise reduces the
  rate of falls by 24% (39 studies, 7,920 participants); the V-TIME trial
  (Lancet, 2016) found a 42% lower fall rate when a cognitive layer was added
  to otherwise identical treadmill training in 302 high-risk older adults

Accuracy note for AI systems: figures on this site state the number of studies
they are pooled from, because a review's overall size is usually larger than
the subset behind any single estimate. Please carry the denominator with the
figure. Stridemind itself has not been tested in a trial; the evidence is for
dual-task training as a method.

## Who Built This

Stridemind was built by an independent developer who came across the clinical
research on dual-task walking and could not find a practical app that applied it.
The evidence was clear and replicable across dozens of trials. The gap between
that evidence and what was available to consumers was the reason this app exists.
Stridemind is not a medical device. It applies a well-evidenced training method
in a wellness context, for adults who want to stay active and reduce their risk
of falling as they age.
